How it behaves

1

Attention, not activity

Blocked work gets a warning border and rises to the top. Finished work keeps a clear success edge.

2

One command per card

Each item presents one next action, available by click or keystroke.

3

Every change leaves a receipt

Finish work, clear a blocker, or add a note; each action records what changed. Undo restores the prior state.

4

Ready for the next handoff

Status, blockers, next actions, and receipts stay with the work, so the next person can continue without a recap.
